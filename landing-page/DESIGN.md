## Overview

Harvey's spacious split hero, serif typography, generous product illustrations, short text and clear CTA inform the composition. Bozon retains its logo and green identity. The visitor should recognise an accountancy workflow, understand the engagement, and request a review.

## Colors

Light pages suit daytime reading in an accountancy practice. Forest green anchors the interactive demonstration and engagement. Pale green distinguishes illustrative work surfaces. Amber is reserved for items requiring review.

## Typography

Libre Caslon Display provides the high-contrast editorial serif character of the chosen reference. Geist carries navigation, explanations and workflow data. Headings are sentence case, body copy brief. No marketing text in monospace. Body copy is 18px on desktop and 16px on mobile; controls are 16px; captions and disclaimers are 14px. Illustrative data never falls below 13px. Preserve these role sizes across breakpoints and let content wrap rather than shrinking it. The user explicitly requested larger, more readable typography after reviewing the initial site.

## Layout

Maximum content width 1320px. Desktop hero pairs a large headline with a concise explanation and CTA. A wide workflow demonstration follows. Sections alternate full-width illustrations, a horizontal trust strip, split layouts and a dark engagement panel. Below 760px, stack split layouts and keep controls comfortably tappable.

## Elevation & Depth

Use borders for real application surfaces and restrained offset shadows for document sheets only. No ornamental glass or gradients. Keep all substantive content visible without animation.

## Shapes

Near-square controls, softly rounded illustrative software surfaces and hairline rules. Avoid a page composed of repeated cards.

## Components

Workflow tabs support keyboard navigation. The capacity calculator labels every assumption and uses native range inputs. FAQs use native details. Contact opens the visitor's email application and does not imply a booking is confirmed. A short hero entrance respects reduced motion.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Give each section one job and keep supporting prose short.
- Clearly label fictional workflow examples and potential savings.
- Describe former employers as founder experience, never clients or endorsements.
- Preserve keyboard focus, responsive layouts and AA text contrast.
- Do not invent testimonials, metrics, founder roles, certifications or integrations.
- Do not use em dashes in marketing copy.
